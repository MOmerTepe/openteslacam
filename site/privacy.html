HexCam

Privacy

Last changed 25 November 2026.

HexCam has no account, no analytics and no advertising. Your footage, and the vehicle data inside it, are read on your Mac and stay there. Nothing is uploaded.

What the app reads

Only the folders you choose: the drive or folder you open, the library folder you pick for imports, and the folder you export into. macOS enforces this; the app runs in the system's sandbox and cannot read anything else.

What it keeps

Your settings, layouts and marks, and a list of the events you have imported, are kept on your Mac. They are not sent anywhere.

When it uses the internet

  • The map. The map is Apple Maps. To draw it, your Mac asks Apple for the map of the area the footage was recorded in, as any map on a Mac does. Apple's privacy policy covers that request.
  • Updates. When it opens, the app asks GitHub once whether a newer version exists. The request carries the app's name and version and nothing about you or your footage. It can be switched off in Settings.
  • Purchases. Buying Vehicle Data goes through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app learns only whether the purchase was made.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees the address a request comes from.
